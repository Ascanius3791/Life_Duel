How to play

The board

Red owns the left fifth, Blue the right fifth. The neutral battlefield in the middle holds grey cells. Spend your points on patterns from your arsenal and drop them into your own area.

Life as usual…

  • A living cell with 2 or 3 living neighbours survives, otherwise it dies.
  • A dead cell with exactly 3 living neighbours is born.
  • All colours count as "living" for these rules.

…but with colours

  • A newborn cell takes the colour that has more parents: red vs blue.
  • Grey parents help a cell to be born but never vote.
  • A tie (e.g. 1 red, 1 blue, 1 grey or 3 grey) produces a grey cell.
  • Contagion variant: surviving cells are re-coloured every generation by the same vote (tie → grey).

Winning

  • Wipe out every cell of the opponent's colour.
  • Otherwise, when the generation limit is reached, more cells wins. The limit can also be set to ∞.
  • If the whole board repeats a previous state, the game ends early and the average number of cells over one cycle decides.

Controls

  • Drag a card onto your area, or click a card and then click the board repeatedly. Esc or right-click stops placing.
  • R / Shift+R rotate, F mirror left/right, V mirror up/down. The mouse wheel rotates while placing.
  • Right-click a placed pattern to remove it and get the points back.
  • A folds the arsenal away or brings it back.
  • During the battle: Space play/pause, → single step, +/− speed, M max speed, F focus mode (maximised board, Esc leaves it).
